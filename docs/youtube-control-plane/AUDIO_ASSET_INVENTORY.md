## Why none of it ships

Both meme packs carry a `README.txt` naming their origin outright:

> `Source: https://www.myinstants.com/en/search/?name=meme`

They were scraped from a public soundboard site. There is no licence file, no rights field in
the manifest, and no per-sound attribution — only the URL each file came from. The filenames
say plainly what most of them are: *Among Us role reveal sound*, *Death sound (Fortnite)*,
*Valorant spike plant*, *spiderman meme song*, *JOJO SONG*, *OP (Gangnam Style)*. That is game,
film and music audio owned by Innersloth, Epic, Riot, Sony, and YG Entertainment.

The scanner reached the same conclusion independently and marked 2,202 of them `AVOID` with
reasons like `"explicitly a meme clip"`.

The publishing rule this channel already runs on:

> **If permission is unknown and cannot be substantiated, the asset does not ship.**

That rule was applied once before. `BG_SneakyMusic` had been mixed into all ten Batch 001
videos as a music bed; it had no licence evidence either, and it was removed before release
rather than published on a bet. Re-introducing scraped audio now would undo that decision.

**"USABLE" in the table above is a judgement about CONTENT, not about rights.** It means the
scanner found a clean, short, non-vocal sound — not that anyone is permitted to publish it.
Sixty files being musically usable does not make them licensed.

## What the channel actually uses

Every sound in the published videos is generated by `tools/make-sfx.mjs` — fifteen procedural
effects synthesised from oscillators and noise, with no sample source. Original work, nothing
to license, and it is why the SFX pass has never needed a rights review.

Music is currently **none**. `MUSIC_PLAN` is a partial map and an absent entry means the
episode runs dry, so the rights-safe answer is the one you get by doing nothing.

---

## If you want meme audio later

Two routes, both real:

1. **Licence it.** A stock library with a commercial licence covering YouTube monetisation.
   Record the licence alongside the asset in `audio_source.provenance` and the risk drops out
   of `UNKNOWN` honestly.
2. **Synthesise it.** `make-sfx.mjs` already produces the channel's whole effect vocabulary.
   A stinger or a comedy hit is well within what it can generate, and it stays original.

What is not a route: shipping the scraped packs and hoping no claim lands. A copyright claim
on a monetised Short takes the revenue for that video and counts against the channel.
